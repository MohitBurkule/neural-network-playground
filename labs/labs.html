🧠 Main Playground

The unified deep-net playground — design multi-layer networks, switch datasets, try optimizers, run adversarial attacks, machine unlearning, 3-D weight visualization, interpretability tools, and experiments all in one place.

Flagship

Deep Learning

🖼️ Convolutional Neural Network

Build and train a CNN from scratch on tiny images. Watch live convolutional filters and feature maps evolve as the network learns.

CNN 🔮 Transformer

Self-attention visualized on a toy sequence task. Inspect attention heads, positional encodings, and how context flows through each layer.

Attention 🔁 Recurrent Neural Network

Elman RNN trained with backpropagation-through-time (BPTT). A hidden-state heat-strip shows how sequence memory builds up step by step.

RNN · BPTT 🗜️ Autoencoder

Compress data to a 2-D latent space and decode it back. Explore the learned manifold interactively by clicking anywhere in latent space.

Latent Space ⚔️ Generative Adversarial Network

Generator and discriminator locked in a minimax game over 2-D distributions. Watch the generator gradually fool the discriminator in real time.

GAN 🌫️ Diffusion Model

DDPM forward noising progressively destroys structure; the trained network reverses the process, denoising back to clean 2-D samples.

DDPM 📝 Word2Vec

Train skip-gram embeddings on a small corpus. Explore nearest-neighbor relationships and solve word analogies in the projected embedding space.

Embeddings 🎲 Bayesian Neural Network

Quantify predictive uncertainty using MC-dropout and deep ensembles. The uncertainty envelope widens visibly in regions with sparse training data.

Uncertainty

Classic ML

🌳 Decision Tree & Random Forest

Grow decision trees of variable depth and aggregate them into a random forest. The live decision boundary updates as you tune depth and tree count.

Trees ✂️ Support Vector Machine

Experiment with linear, RBF, and polynomial kernels. The maximum-margin hyperplane and support vectors are drawn live as you adjust C and γ.

SVM · Kernels 📈 Linear / Logistic / Naive Bayes

Compare generalized linear models side by side on 2-D data: linear regression, logistic regression, and Gaussian Naive Bayes — all with live fits.

GLM 🌊 Gaussian Process

Click to add observations and watch the posterior mean and confidence ribbon update instantly. Tune the kernel to change smoothness and length-scale.

GP · Kernel 🔵 k-Means / DBSCAN / GMM

Three clustering algorithms on the same 2-D canvas. Adjust k, epsilon, and min-samples to see how each method handles noise and non-convex shapes.

Clustering 📉 PCA & t-SNE

Project high-dimensional data down to 2-D with PCA or t-SNE. Adjust perplexity and iterations and watch clusters emerge in the scatter plot.

Dim Reduction 🗺️ Self-Organizing Map

Kohonen SOM lattice folds and unfolds over your data as training progresses, creating a topology-preserving 2-D map of the input distribution.

Other

🕹️ Reinforcement Learning Gridworld

Q-learning and SARSA on a configurable grid with walls, rewards, and penalties. Watch value estimates and the greedy policy converge episode by episode.

Q-Learning · SARSA 🧩 Hopfield Network

Store binary patterns as energy minima and demonstrate associative memory recall — corrupted inputs converge back to the nearest stored memory.

Associative Memory 🧬 Genetic Algorithm

Evolve a population toward optimal fitness on configurable landscapes. Tune selection pressure, mutation rate, and crossover to see evolutionary dynamics.

Evolutionary

Sampling & Optimization

🎰 Multi-Armed Bandit

Explore vs. exploit: compare ε-greedy, optimistic initial values, UCB1, and Thompson sampling on a k-armed bandit, watching cumulative regret and optimal-action rates.

Exploration 📉 Optimizer Visualizer

Race SGD, Momentum, Nesterov, Adagrad, RMSProp, and Adam down 2-D loss surfaces (Rosenbrock, saddle, Beale, ravine…) and watch their trajectories diverge.

Gradient Descent 🔡 Markov Chain Text

Train an n-gram (char or word) Markov model on any corpus and generate new text, with a live next-token distribution and temperature control.

N-gram 🎲 MCMC Sampling

Sample 2-D distributions with Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs, and HMC-lite. Watch the chain explore the density and the sample histogram converge to the target.
