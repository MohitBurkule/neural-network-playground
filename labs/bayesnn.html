How it works: MC-Dropout keeps dropout active at inference time and runs many forward passes — the spread of outputs captures epistemic uncertainty. Deep Ensemble trains N networks from different random initialisations; disagreement between members reveals uncertainty. In both cases the shaded band widens in the yellow gap region where no data exists, and shrinks where training points are dense. Click anywhere on the plot to inject a new point into the gap — watch the bands collapse there within seconds of training.
